## 2026-08-11 - [Custom Floating Toasts with Proper Layering]
**Learning:** To deliver a premium, non-disruptive user experience, browser-native copy alerts can be replaced with an inline visual state transition or a styled floating toast notification on the triggering page. To build accessible and robust custom toast notifications, include `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"` attributes on the element, set a high z-index (such as 3000) to ensure they render above standard modal overlays and backdrop elements (which default to z-index 2000), and handle rapid, succession copying gracefully by saving and clearing previous timeouts via `clearTimeout`.
**Action:** For future copy actions, always replace disruptive browser-level alerts with a styled custom toast component utilizing this robust, accessible structure and high-priority layering approach.

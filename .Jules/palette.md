## 2024-11-23 - Custom Accessible Toast Copy-Alerts & Icon-only Button UX
**Learning:** Browser-native copy `alert()` calls disrupt flow and feel unpolished. Custom floating toast notifications should be used instead, equipped with `role="status"` and `aria-live="polite"` attributes to ensure they are fully accessible to screen readers. Rapid clicking on copy actions can result in race conditions that hide notifications prematurely unless any previous `setTimeout` handle is systematically tracked and cleared. Additionally, standard icon-only buttons (like notifications, logouts, and copy triggers) must always declare an explicit `aria-label` attribute, and child icons must hide themselves using `aria-hidden="true"` to avoid duplicate or unlabelled readouts.
**Action:** For all future copy-to-clipboard interactions, implement a localized, non-disruptive floating toast element with proper accessibility attributes, clear previous timer handlers using `clearTimeout`, and apply standard `aria-label` attributes to icon-only buttons.
